
*** Creation of CESI (Climate Exposure and Sensitivity Index) 

Aggregates Air Quality, Level of Informality (Slum Index) and Flood Risk into a CESI score per EA 

Air Quality & Level of Informality from single sources 

Flood Risk is made up of 
- total rainfall exposure (in XYZ file)
- mean elevation 
- mean slope 
- mean NDVI
