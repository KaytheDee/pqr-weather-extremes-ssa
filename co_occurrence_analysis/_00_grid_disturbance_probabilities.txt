
*** Grid Disturbance Probabilities 

This section basically visualizes (as bar plots) the co-occurrence rates for each extreme weather combination and outage (1+hour, 8+hour) or under voltage (1+hour, 4+hour). 

------ 

The Accra Aca (right?) weather station had lots of missing wind data and it was positioned in the southern central portion of Greater Accra. 

Because this area had high CESI EAs, we did not want to lose this data, by filtering for only the EAs that had ALL datasets (temp, precip, wind and lightning). 

We therefore settled on two datasets: 
TPL (EAs with temp, precip and lightning data) 
& 
TPLW (EAs with temp, precip, wind and lightning data) 

------ 

I had earlier calculated the weather combinations (among each) with the highest occurrences (based on % occurrence in that dataset). 
And selected the highest combinations from the two datasets. 

For instance, if I had the percentage occurrence for T days to be 35% in the TPL dataset, but 32% in the TPLW dataset, I chose the T in the TPL dataset. 
The next combination chosen would be the one with the next highest percentage (among the 2 datasets).
