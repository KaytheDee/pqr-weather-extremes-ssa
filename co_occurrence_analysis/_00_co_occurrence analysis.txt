
*** Co-occurrence Analysis 

Here, I calculate the co-occurrence of the various extreme weather combinations (the top 11 to be exact) with outages (1+hour, 8+hour) and under voltages (1+hour and 4+hour). 

I run this for 3 different extreme weather severity levels: 
- 90th 
- 95th and 
- 99th percentiles 

I also run the level of statistical significance for each (using the Chi squared independence test)
